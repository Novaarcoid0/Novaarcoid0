🔒 Having trouble connecting?

If the predictor isn't working at school/work, it may be due to firewall restrictions. Here are some options:

  • Use on mobile data: Try using your phone's data connection instead of the school/work WiFi
  • Access from home: The tool will work on unrestricted networks
  • Ask IT department: Request access to api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov
  • Alternative: Visit NOAA's official site directly
